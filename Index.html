WHO WE ARE

About InAmigos Foundation

Creating meaningful change through collective action

InAmigos Foundation is a Section 8 registered non-profit organization founded on September 23, 2020 by Mr. Govind Shukla, Founder & CEO.

The foundation works toward community development and social empowerment through initiatives in education, women empowerment, environmental sustainability, animal welfare, community support and skill development.

InAmigos believes that meaningful social change becomes possible when individuals, volunteers and organizations come together and turn awareness into action.
